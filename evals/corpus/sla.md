---
title: Service Level Agreement
date: 2026-01-20
---
Monthly uptime commitment: 99.9% on the Growth plan and 99.95% on the Enterprise plan. Starter has no contractual uptime commitment.

Service credits: if monthly uptime drops below the commitment, the customer gets a 10% credit on that month's fee. Below 99.0% the credit is 25%. Credits must be requested within 30 days and never exceed one month's fee.

Support first-response targets: P1 (production down) — 1 hour for Enterprise and 4 hours for Growth; P2 — 8 business hours. Planned maintenance is announced 72 hours ahead and does not count as downtime.
