---
title: Remote Work Policy
date: 2025-09-15
---
Brightwave is remote-first. Core collaboration hours are 11:00–15:00 CET; outside them people work flexibly.

Every employee gets a one-time home office stipend of $500 and $40 per month towards internet costs. Employees who prefer an office can use a coworking space; the company covers up to $250 per month.

Working from another country for more than 30 days a year requires HR approval for tax reasons.
