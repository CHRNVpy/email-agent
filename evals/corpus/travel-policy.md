---
title: Travel Policy
date: 2025-10-01
---
All business travel is booked through the company travel platform at least 14 days in advance when possible.

Hotels are capped at $220 per night, or $300 per night in New York, San Francisco and London. International trips need written approval from your manager and the department head.

Rail is preferred over flights for trips under 4 hours. Travel insurance is provided by the company for all business trips.
