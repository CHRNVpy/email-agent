---
title: Support Playbook
date: 2026-01-30
---
Severity levels: P1 — production down or data loss; P2 — a major feature is broken with no workaround; P3 — a feature is degraded or has a workaround; P4 — questions and feature requests.

P1 tickets are escalated immediately to the on-call engineer through PagerDuty and the customer gets updates every hour.

Refunds and service credits: support agents may approve up to $1,000; anything above $1,000 needs approval from the Finance team. Every refund must be logged in the billing system with the ticket number.
