---
title: Data Retention & Deletion Policy
date: 2025-11-20
---
Raw event data is kept for 13 months and then deleted automatically. Aggregated metrics are kept for as long as the customer's account is active.

When a customer asks for their data to be deleted, we complete the deletion within 30 days and send a confirmation. Backups are rotated and purged after 35 days, so deleted data disappears from backups within that time.

After an account is closed, all customer data is deleted within 90 days unless the contract says otherwise.
