---
title: Security & Compliance Overview
date: 2026-02-10
---
Brightwave has held a SOC 2 Type II report since 2024 and renews it every year. An independent firm runs a penetration test every year; the summary can be shared under NDA.

Customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Customers choose a data residency region when signing up: EU customers are hosted in Frankfurt (eu-central-1), US customers in Oregon (us-west-2). Data never leaves the chosen region.

Single sign-on with SAML 2.0 and SCIM user provisioning are available on the Growth and Enterprise plans; they are not available on Starter. Audit logs are kept for 12 months.
