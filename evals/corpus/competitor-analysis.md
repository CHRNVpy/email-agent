---
title: "Competitive analysis: Datapulse"
date: 2026-02-25
---
Datapulse is our most frequent competitor in mid-market deals.

Where they win: a cheaper entry plan and a large template library.
Where we win: SSO and audit logs on mid-tier plans (Datapulse only offers SSO on its top plan), better anomaly detection, and EU data residency.

Our win rate against Datapulse in 2025 was 58%. Recommended talk track: lead with security and time-to-insight, not price.
