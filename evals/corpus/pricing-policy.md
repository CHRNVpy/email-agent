---
title: Pricing & Plans Policy
date: 2026-01-15
---
Brightwave Insights is sold per user per month. There are three plans.

**Starter** costs $49 per user per month and includes up to 10 dashboards, daily data refresh and email support.

**Growth** costs $99 per user per month and adds unlimited dashboards, hourly refresh, the anomaly alerts module and priority support.

**Enterprise** is priced individually. It adds a dedicated cluster option, custom data residency and a named customer success manager.

Annual billing gets a 15% discount on Starter and Growth. Registered non-profits and schools get 30% off any plan. Quoted prices are locked for 12 months from the contract start date. Sales may approve discounts up to 20% on their own; anything above that needs approval from the VP of Sales.
