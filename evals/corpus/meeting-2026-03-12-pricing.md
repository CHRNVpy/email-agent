---
title: "Meeting notes: Pricing review"
date: 2026-03-12
---
Attendees: CEO, VP of Sales, Head of Finance, Product lead.

Decisions:
1. The Growth plan goes from $89 to $99 per user per month for new customers starting 1 May 2026.
2. Existing Growth customers keep $89 for 12 more months, then move to the new price.
3. Starter stays at $49; no change to Enterprise pricing.
4. Anomaly Alerts will be included in Growth rather than sold as an add-on, to justify the increase.

Action items: Finance updates the price book by 1 April; Marketing announces the change to customers on 15 April.
