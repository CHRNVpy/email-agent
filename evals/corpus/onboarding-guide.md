---
title: Customer Onboarding Guide
date: 2025-12-01
---
Every new customer goes through a 90-day onboarding programme.

Week 1: a kickoff call with the customer success manager to agree on goals, name the admin users and schedule training sessions.

Weeks 2–3: data connectors are set up and the first three dashboards are built together with the customer's analysts.

By day 30 the customer has a written success plan. Day 60 is a usage review; day 90 is an executive business review where we compare results with the goals from the kickoff.
