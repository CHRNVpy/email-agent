---
title: Expense Policy
date: 2025-10-01
---
Employees are reimbursed for reasonable business expenses. Meals while travelling are covered up to $60 per day. Client dinners need a manager's approval above $150 per person.

Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy; longer flights may be booked in premium economy. Receipts are required for any expense above $25.

Expenses must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days; later submissions need the CFO's approval.
