---
title: Refund Policy
date: 2025-11-03
---
Monthly subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of the first payment. Renewals of monthly plans are not refundable.

Annual subscriptions can be cancelled within 60 days of the start date. The refund is prorated: the customer pays for the months used and gets the rest back. After 60 days annual plans are non-refundable, but the customer can downgrade at renewal.

Add-ons (extra connectors, premium support) are not refundable once activated. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days.
