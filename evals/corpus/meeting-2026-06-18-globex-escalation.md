date: 2026-06-18
---
Globex (Enterprise customer) complained that their executive dashboards take more than 20 seconds to load during month-end close.

Agreed with the customer:
- a 30-day trial of a dedicated cluster at no extra cost;
- a 15% service credit on the June invoice as a goodwill gesture;
- weekly check-in calls with their data team until load times are under 5 seconds.

Owner on our side: the Globex customer success manager, with support from the Platform team.
